## Business Analysis Approach

My approach is:

**Data → Cleaning → Analysis → KPI Calculation → Visualization → Insight → Business Recommendation**

I separate my analysis into three areas:

- **What the data shows**
- **What the data may suggest**
- **What requires further investigation**

This helps me avoid making business conclusions that are not supported by the available data.
